# Source: Caught #1

- **Document:** "Grandparent Scammer Sentenced to Federal Prison", press release
- **Office:** U.S. Attorney's Office, District of Rhode Island
- **Date:** Thursday, February 29, 2024 (sentencing was the day before, Wednesday, February 28, 2024)
- **URL:** https://www.justice.gov/usao-ri/pr/grandparent-scammer-sentenced-federal-prison
- **Status:** federal government work, public domain (17 U.S.C. 105). Quotes may be shown word for word.

## Facts used, with the release's wording
| Fact | Release wording |
| --- | --- |
| A New York City man traveled to pick up cash, including in Rhode Island | "A New York City man who traveled to various U.S. locations, including Rhode Island, to pick-up thousands of dollars in cash" |
| Victims were seniors | "seniors who had become the unsuspecting victims of so called 'grandparent scams'" |
| Who the callers posed as | "Posing as the victim's grandchild, a relative, a lawyer, or other individual" |
| The story they told | "arrested or incarcerated in connection with a car accident" and "money for bail or related legal expenses" |
| The gag order | "members of the conspiracy falsely told victims that a 'gag order' was in place in their relative's legal matter" |
| Cash was shipped | "as directed by the scammers, had been shipped by the victims to designated addresses" |
| Start of the investigation | January 2021, New Haven, CT police received "reports from out-of-state police departments"; "A broader investigation by the New Haven Police and the FBI" followed |
| Age | 38 (as of the release, Feb 2024) |
| Plea | "pleaded guilty on February 24, 2023, to charges of conspiracy to commit mail fraud and mail fraud" |
| Sentence | 30 months of incarceration, three years of federal supervised release |
| Restitution | "ordered to pay a total of $867,149 to victims of the scam" |

The release does not say how the courier himself was identified, the number of victims, or any single victim's loss. The script doesn't claim any of those.
