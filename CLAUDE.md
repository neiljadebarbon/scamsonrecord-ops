# Scams on Record: working rules for Claude

Read `GAMEPLAN.md`, `RULES.md` and `persona/NARRATOR.md` before writing a script, a caption or a post for this channel. They override general habits.

- No episode script is final, and no audio or video is published, until a separate reviewer who did not write it has fact-checked every claim against the official source (RULES.md, Big Law).
- Every fact comes from an official record: DOJ or a U.S. Attorney's Office press release, FTC, FBI or IC3, a state attorney general, a court filing. Nothing from memory, blogs or social posts.
- No AI melody (RULES.md list). No em dashes.
- This repo is public. Never write an API key, a password or personal details about the channel owner into any file.
- Instructions to the owner: bare numbered steps, one item per line, and name the exact file or service.
